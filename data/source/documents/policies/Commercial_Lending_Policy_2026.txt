COMMERCIAL LENDING POLICY 2026
Version: 2026.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes minimum credit standards for commercial lending facilities,
including working capital facilities, term loans, and revolving credit facilities.

Final credit decisions remain subject to authorized credit officer approval.

2. DEBT SERVICE COVERAGE

POL_DSCR_001

Borrowers requesting working capital facilities must maintain a minimum Debt Service
Coverage Ratio (DSCR) of 1.25.

A DSCR below 1.25 constitutes a policy exception and requires documented credit
officer review.

3. LEVERAGE

POL_LEVERAGE_001

Debt-to-EBITDA should not exceed 4.00 for standard commercial borrowers.

A Debt-to-EBITDA ratio above 4.00 constitutes a leverage policy exception.

4. FINANCIAL DOCUMENTATION

POL_DOC_001

The borrower must provide audited financial statements for the latest completed
financial year.

If audited financial statements are unavailable, the application must be classified
as having insufficient financial documentation until an authorized exception is
recorded.

5. BANKING RELATIONSHIP

POL_RELATIONSHIP_001

Working capital borrowers should have a minimum banking relationship of two years
unless an authorized credit officer approves an exception.

6. RELATED-PARTY TRANSACTIONS

POL_RELATED_PARTY_001

Material transactions involving a borrower, its directors, affiliates, subsidiaries,
or other related parties require enhanced credit review.

The analyst must evaluate the transaction's business purpose, size, frequency, and
potential impact on borrower liquidity.

7. LARGE OR UNUSUAL TRANSACTIONS

POL_TRANSACTION_001

Large, unusual, or insufficiently explained transactions identified during account
analysis require enhanced review before final credit approval.

The presence of an unusual transaction does not by itself establish fraud or require
automatic rejection.

8. CREDIT DECISION AUTHORITY

POL_AUTHORITY_001

Automated models, analytical systems, and artificial intelligence systems may support
credit analysis but must not independently approve or reject a commercial credit
application.

Final approval, rejection, or exception authority remains with authorized human
credit officers.
